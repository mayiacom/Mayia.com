Your inputs, privacy, and AI limits

When you generate, your audience, topic, unique angle, format, and level are sent to the AI provider through Mayia’s server. Mayia does not store those prompts on its server. Saved projects stay in this browser’s local storage until you clear them. Do not enter confidential or sensitive information.

AI can be wrong, generic, or out of date. It cannot validate demand, verify your expertise, or guarantee sales. Review the output and independently verify factual, legal, health, financial, and safety claims.
